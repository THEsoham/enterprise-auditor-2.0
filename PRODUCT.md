# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vanilla HTML, CSS, JavaScript (ES6+) frontend with Python FastAPI backend and ChromaDB / LangChain hybrid retrieval.

## Users

Enterprise legal counsel, procurement executives, contract managers, and deal attorneys conducting pre-signature risk audits on MSAs, vendor contracts, and enterprise agreements under time constraints.

## Product Purpose

Deliver an autonomous, zero-hallucination contract risk audit that flags fatal liabilities, unilateral cancellation terms, missing standard protections, and asymmetric clauses with verbatim cited evidence, dual-agent verification, and instant redline negotiation copy.

## Positioning

Unlike generic LLM summary tools that produce unverifiable hallucinated commentary, Enterprise Auditor 2.0 enforces a dual-agent courtroom cross-examination architecture: a Risk Prosecutor identifies exposure while an Independent Skeptic Verifier cross-checks exact PDF coordinates and verbatim quotes to achieve 100% verified consensus.

## Operating Context

High-stakes commercial deals, procurement reviews, vendor risk assessments, and executive deal desk approvals on desktop and tablet screens.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Instant multi-format document ingestion (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD).
- 100-Point Dynamic Contract Safety Score with collapsible breakdown accordion.
- 2-Stage Adversarial Multi-Agent Debate Arena (Stage 1: Extracted claims & custom probe box; Stage 2: Animated FOR vs AGAINST debate panels with verbatim citation and consensus stamp).
- Asymmetric Clause Detection (Vendor vs Customer rights balance).
- Side-by-Side Clause Market Comparison.
- Executive Redline & Negotiation Memo Generator with clipboard copy and print/download.
- Interactive Obligations Timeline & Entity Scanner.

## Brand Commitments

Executive-grade legal-tech aesthetic: razor-sharp typography, high contrast, elegant dark-slate palette with luminous amber/indigo/emerald accents, crisp spatial layout, zero fluff, instant micro-interactions.

## Evidence on Hand

Real contract audit backend pipeline (`AuditPipeline`), BM25 + dense semantic retrieval, dual-agent debate history, and enterprise cloud MSA sample documents (`sample_contract.pdf`).

## Product Principles

1. **Verbatim Evidence Grounding**: Every identified risk must show the exact contractual clause citation.
2. **Progressive Disclosure**: High-level verdict and key metrics visible at a glance; deep analytical views opened on intentional click.
3. **Adversarial Transparency**: Surface both the prosecution argument and defense cross-examination openly so counsel can evaluate nuance.
4. **Actionable Remediation**: Every identified defect must pair with an immediate, copyable redline edit.
5. **Impeccable Craft**: Fluid transitions, crystal-clear information hierarchy, high accessibility, and zero friction.

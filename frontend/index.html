How the Contract Safety Score is Calculated

Every contract starts at a baseline of 100 points. Deductions are systematically applied based on identified risk severity and standard protection omissions:

Deal-Breakers
-15 points per issue
Unlimited liability, broad indemnification, one-sided termination
Watch-Outs
-5 points per issue
Auto-renewal traps, vague warranties, short cure periods
Minor Terms
-2 points per issue
Notice procedure specifics, audit record retention timelines
Missing Clauses
-4 points per gap
Missing mutual NDA, data return guarantees, force majeure
A score of 80+ is low risk, 60–79 requires standard revisions, and below 60 indicates high-risk terms requiring legal renegotiation.
